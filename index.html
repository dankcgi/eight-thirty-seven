Why does this exist?

During the covid years, I lived with a guy called Watson who had an alarm for 8:37 PM, every night. He had set it some time before we lived together to remind him to do some task, and, after completing said task, had not unset it. It remained active for months, and given how everyone went a bit cuckoo during that time, it going off at that time each evening became a cherished event in the house.

This site answers the question that alarm raised, every second of every day, using nothing but HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
